# Part A. Reference audit, digital measurement assessment and mathematical detail

## S1. Scope and protocol history

The six revision priorities are addressed by an independent pretrained IC-U-Net deployment, a real reference-construction audit, 20-person calibration plus all-40 cross-fitting, explicit measurement-assessment contributions, removal of ECG/BCI evidence from the core argument, and bandlimited guard mechanism controls. The released EEGdenoiseNet windows do **not** identify their latent neural impurity. Measured construction disagreement is neither an estimate nor an upper bound on δ. ERP/task content cannot be tested in a window pool without event identities, channel provenance, pre-construction parents, and simultaneous peripheral recordings.

The follow-up protocol was specified after the earlier 20/10/10 results were seen. Primary fitting now uses 10 development people, with 20 calibration and the same 10 evaluation people. This is exploratory reuse of the cohort; numerical fitting isolation does not turn it into a fresh confirmatory study. The broad spatial probe followed an audit of localized montage attenuation. Fivefold cross-fitting and partition sensitivity are follow-up diagnostics. Pooled decoder-dependent cross-fitted scores have no exact split-conformal guarantee.

- Development: 30, 8, 24, 39, 36, 1, 29, 23, 6, 25
- Calibration: 21, 17, 31, 32, 22, 19, 12, 26, 11, 3, 27, 4, 5, 40, 2, 13, 18, 20, 15, 28
- Evaluation: 7, 14, 37, 10, 9, 16, 38, 33, 34, 35

The initial configuration snapshot is `results/revision/config_before_description_clarification.json`. An inherited textual score description incorrectly said eight nonparent operations; it was corrected to match the **seven explicitly listed calibration candidates actually used throughout**. No setting, split, candidate, endpoint, or numerical algorithm changed. The two classical fits and all digital assessments were rerun after this clarification; old and new operator tensors match exactly.

## S2. Published independent model

IC-U-Net: Chuang et al., NeuroImage 263, 119586 (2022), doi: [10.1016/j.neuroimage.2022.119586](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2022.119586). Original author repository commit `7f4f27dbf79c0909a0993f680209cf24c32f7791`; 2,669,854 parameters; strict state loading; no ERP fine-tuning.

- Architecture SHA-256: `be5a7a9350f36f06d35f76d8ab1d84d535d2a85441d584413785bb1044c427b0`.
- Checkpoint SHA-256: `591d2a7819c4ddcd6280198645aae59c80e40c280a4ec7ac368068c33c2de75e`.

## S3. Real reference-construction audit

All 4514 centered, unit-RMS, 512-sample/256-Hz EEG windows were audited. Butterworth alternatives are fourth-order, forward–backward, on the released two-second windows; boundary distortion is possible. Fourier alternatives are orthogonal projections, not ground-truth references. These constructions may specify different measurands. Treat their convex hull as an admissible neural class only with separate application-specific justification. Under that membership assumption, convexity gives δ ≤ max_j ||T−T_j||; for this normalized corpus family the bound is 46.37% of reference RMS. This is a data-derived conditional bound, not a physiological calibration of δ.

```latex
Additional evidence may constrain only some directions. In coordinates scaled to empirical MSE, suppose $L\Delta=b$ is independently established and $\|\Delta-L^\dagger b\|_2\leq r$. With $P_0=I-L^\dagger L$,
\begin{equation}
D_N=D_T+2v^\top L^\dagger b\ \pm\ 2r\|P_0v\|_2.
\label{eq:partial}
\end{equation}
Every feasible bias is $L^\dagger b+h$, $h\in\ker L$; alignment with $P_0v$ attains the extremes. One endpoint can therefore constrain a ranking without identifying the whole waveform. Parent-relative changes below do not measure $b$ for an unknown cortical target.
```

```latex
A known-source control separately verifies the missing implication. Orthogonal unit-RMS modes $g_b,g_u,g_a$ define $N=Bg_b+Ug_u$, $A=Vg_u+Cg_a$, $T_\alpha=Bg_b+(1-\alpha)Ug_u$, and $\widetilde A_\alpha=A+\alpha Ug_u$. ## S14. Full derivations and diagnostic detail moved from the main text
```

```latex
Without restrictions on sources, $(N+H)+(A-H)=Y$ for every admissible $H$. If one observation permits neural targets $n_0,n_1$, every estimate $z$ obeys
\begin{equation}
\max\{\|z-n_0\|_2^2,\|z-n_1\|_2^2\}
\geq\tfrac14\|n_0-n_1\|_2^2.
\label{eq:nonid}
\end{equation}
The triangle inequality makes at least one distance no smaller than half the target separation. This is a standard nonidentifiability statement for the specified source class. Spatial information, reference channels, independence, or validated source models can restrict that class. Spectral overlap alone does not prohibit multichannel separation; prediction of $T$ under a fixed joint law remains well defined.


Increasing capacity cannot validate the target relation. Conditional-expectation orthogonality gives
\begin{equation}
R_N(\E[T\mid X])-R_N(\E[N\mid X])
=\|\E[\Delta\mid X]\|^2.
\label{eq:optimum}
\end{equation}
This identifies an objective mismatch when the reference bias is predictable from the input. It does not establish that a released dataset has that bias. These elementary identities are used here to delimit claims, not presented as new mathematical results.


The follow-up split assigns 10 people to development, 20 to calibration, and 10 to evaluation, keeping both tasks together. It retains the earlier ten evaluation people and reallocates ten of the original twenty development people to calibration. This revision was specified after earlier cohort outcomes were inspected; it is exploratory and does not constitute fresh confirmatory validation. Development people alone fit the pooled operators, scalers, and decoders. The added reference recipe fits ICA separately and without event labels on each recording, as an explicitly adaptive construction. Historical identities in denoiser source pools are unavailable, preventing a complete pretrained-model participant crosswalk.


Broad-direction IC-U-Net gains average 0.682 for N170 and 0.882 for P3; their 5th--95th percentiles are 0.521--0.948 and 0.562--1.242. FemtoEOGClean gives 0.757 and 0.103, with ranges 0.141--1.216 and $-0.123$--0.341. This dispersion would be concealed by a single mean. The broad mapping-only N170 gain is 0.829. For the localized N170 direction, mapping alone gives $-0.053$ and IC-U-Net $-0.037$; that attenuation cannot be attributed solely to the network. Probe geometry and deployment mapping must accompany the gain claim.

The smooth guard preserves the parent P3 contrast but gives 64.69\% primary BA, a change of $-0.25$ points ($[-2.31,+1.59]$), so a residual deficit is not established in this split. To isolate the earlier guard mechanism, we retain the original twenty-development-person decoder and its unchanged ten-person test set. Its P3 deficit is 3.56 points for the rectangular guard, 3.59 for the smooth guard, and 3.63 for the entirely bandlimited variant. The latter two remove the correction's step-edge/high-frequency explanation, while leaving a deficit for that particular decoder. They do not establish lost cortical information. Publishing participant/channel provenance, event labels, simultaneous peripheral recordings, and pre-construction parents would make a stronger purity assessment possible.

The independent ERP assessment adds evidence unavailable to that score. Known-direction response, scalp voltage, prediction, and reference-channel controls address different properties. The endpoint guard proves that matching a mean leaves other directions unconstrained; the bandlimited variants test its edge explanation directly. Decoder and montage controls prevent changes caused by deployment geometry or classification training from being labeled cortical loss. ## 1. What the new experiments identify
```

The new model comparisons test whether synthetic reconstruction performance establishes preservation on independent task recordings. They do **not** identify the cause of every deployment error. A separate intervention changes only the training targets of the same affine learner on the same mixtures, with the same regularization and held-out inputs. That intervention identifies a target-definition effect on **digital output**, conditional on this design. Neither experiment recovers pure cortical voltage or quantifies actual impurity of EEGdenoiseNet's released references.

The minimally processed parent is a comparator. Its P3 can contain task-related peripheral voltage. A smaller contrast need not mean less cortical activity, and a larger contrast need not mean greater accuracy. Dataset labels and ICLabel probabilities are not direct source-purity measurements.

## 5. A target-only intervention with an exact coefficient identity

Three 512-input, 512-output affine ridge fits share the same inputs, regularization and learner. Only their targets change: parent, brain-only recipe, or conservative recipe. Each task uses 20000 fit and 4000 validation mixtures from the ten development people. Trial **indices** are split into fixed halves within person before mixture sampling; fit/validation EOG windows come from distinct public source pools. Neighboring raw epochs can overlap in time, so this is not a claim of fully independent raw-sample or participant validation. The ten evaluation people remain absent from fitting. There is no ERP-validation hyperparameter search: eta is fixed at 0.001×trace(XcᵀXc)/512.

Let Xc and Tc be column-centered matrices. Minimizing ‖Xc W−Tc‖F²+eta‖W‖F² gives W=(XcᵀXc+eta I)⁻¹XcᵀTc and b=mean(T)−mean(X)W. Subtraction for targets T1,T0 proves

**ΔW=(XcᵀXc+eta I)⁻¹Xcᵀ(T1−T0)c**, and **Δb=mean(T1−T0)−mean(X)ΔW**.

The saved float64 designs and targets independently reproduce every coefficient with zero matrix discrepancy. The identity makes explicit which input-correlated target changes propagate into the fit. Input/target normalization uses the same input-derived standard deviation for every target. At deployment the physical output is yW+SD(y)b, followed by the same baseline operation. Thus the compared held-out digital outputs differ only through the fitted target coefficients, not a target-specific scaling or input.

## 9. ICA's five-of-ten coverage: exact rank diagnostic

The problematic ICA here is the **preceding pooled EOG-guided ICA operation**, fitted on ten development people. It is distinct from the new per-record ICLabel recipe. Its joint score is the maximum absolute parent-relative change over both tasks and voltage/BA endpoints, divided by illustrative scales 1 µV and 2 BA points. The 90% threshold is calibration order statistic 19 of 20, q=4.375 (physical limits 4.375 µV and 8.75 BA points). Only five of ten evaluation people satisfy that joint limit.

If a threshold had a fixed known 90% probability, Binomial(10,0.9) would give P(K≤5)=0.00163494. A finite calibration threshold is random. Under iid continuous scores, its coverage probability has Beta(19,2) distribution, yielding a Beta-Binomial(10,19,2) count and P(K≤5)=0.00884173. More generally, condition on 30 distinct exchangeable score ranks and uniformly allocate 20 to calibration: K≤5 iff the 19th calibration rank is at most 24, iff at least 19 of the first 24 ranks are calibration. Thus **P(K≤5)=P{Hypergeometric(N=30, successes=20, draws=24)≥19}=0.00884173**. This rank result does not require a light-tailed score distribution.

100000 random reassignments of the actual frozen scores, retaining ties, give 0.00857. This remains rare. Four failed participants are dominated by N170 decoding changes and one by P3 voltage, rather than a pure voltage heavy-tail mechanism. Heavy tails alone cannot invalidate exchangeable ranks. The conditional diagnostic flags a surprising inspected partition; it does not formally test distribution shift, prove nonexchangeability, or rescue the procedure's coverage. Post-hoc inspection, decoder/operator fitting and small evaluation size prevent a prospective validation claim. A rare exchangeable allocation remains possible.

No new data found here resolves that missing relation. Alternative filter disagreement is therefore construction sensitivity, not δ=‖T−N‖. We do not claim all artifact removal is invalid, that overlap alone precludes multichannel source separation, that linear methods universally suffice, or that all deep models are redundant. The TIM scope cover-letter draft explicitly states this boundary and explains the contribution as validation of processing within an electrical measurement chain, rather than a new neural diagnostic or denoising architecture.

# Part C. Paired CNN supervision and clean-input diagnostics

## 1. What the controlled comparison identifies

Within architecture/seed, three fits share the exact float32 input design, initialized parameters and batch-normalization state, minibatch permutations, dropout seed, RMSprop settings, input-derived target scaling and fixed final epoch. Only the supervisory arrays differ. This identifies the effect of target definition **under this training algorithm and budget** on held-out digital outputs. It is not a claim about an optimizer limit, a population causal effect, cortical source purity or the unique cause of historical CNN deployment failure. Parent voltages are comparators with potentially mixed neural/peripheral content.

The same architectures are used as the historical EEGdenoiseNet-trained fits: simple CNN (16,815,552 parameters) and complex residual CNN (8,455,424 parameters), PyTorch translations of pinned public source. These are retrained architecture ports, not the authors' original checkpoints or published leaderboard replication.

## 4. Matched retraining protocol

The first fixed 10,000 rows per task are taken from the saved affine-intervention training designs and pooled equally, for 20,000 training rows. The first 2,000 validation rows per task give 4,000 diagnostic rows. All training voltages and references come from the same ten development participants; the same trial indices, channels and public EOG draws define the three target conditions. Fit/validation trial indices and EOG source windows are separated, but development participants are shared and neighboring raw epochs may overlap. This is not independent-participant or disjoint-raw-sample validation.

Three paired seeds (17, 29, 43), 50 epochs, batch size 128 and RMSprop (learning rate 5e−5, alpha 0.9, epsilon 1e−7, no weight decay) are fixed. **Epoch 50 is primary for every fit**; validation is diagnostic, without early stopping or selecting a target-specific checkpoint. The three seeds are equally averaged at deployment. Results depend on this finite budget; diagnostic losses and full histories remain available and do not prove convergence.

## 8. Endpoint propagation, with residuals retained

For each participant and linear contrast ℓ, let e_t=F_t−T_t on the identical evaluation arrays. Then

**ℓ(F₁−F₀) = ℓ(T₁−T₀) + ℓ(e₁−e₀).**

Adding/subtracting the references proves this elementary identity for any learner. The direct reference shift, observed learned shift and residual are independently scored from saved physical arrays. The empirical contribution is the controlled transmission test across participants and architectures, not a new algebraic identity. A residual is allowed to offset or amplify the direct shift; ratios are avoided when a reference contrast is small. In the contaminated-input control the corresponding uncontaminated targets remain the intended comparison, so the same decomposition includes contamination/deployment error.

### Independent mixing in the known-source endpoint

The earlier known-source control provides a separate logical check (see `src/run_known_source.py`). At alpha=1, T=B*g_b and the constructed artifact is (U+V)*g_u+C*g_a. B is independent of (U,V,C), so these two constructed pools are independent. Their sum is still the same observed Y=B*g_b+(U+V)*g_u+C*g_a. Projection onto g_b recovers T exactly (R_T=0), while the desired source N=B*g_b+U*g_u differs by U*g_u (R_N=1 with unit-RMS modes and U in {−1,+1}). Thus this endpoint does not rely on dependence between the synthetically combined pools. T contains only stipulated neural activity yet omits an intended neural mode: purity alone would not establish completeness. Intermediate alpha values can share U between both pools and need not be independent. These are stipulated source labels and a possibility result, not evidence of real released-label impurity.


## 9. Secondary calibration and ICA fit sensitivity

Calibration has been demoted in the manuscript; full historical envelopes remain in `causal_revision_supplement.md` and `tim_revision_supplement.md`. For fixed development procedure and exchangeable participant scores S_i=max_j,k |d_ijk|/τ_k, the new score's rank among n+1 scores is uniform (ties conservative). Rank ceil((n+1)(1−alpha)) therefore yields marginal simultaneous change coverage within the fixed family; the order statistic is infinite if that rank exceeds n. It is not cortical-error coverage or conditional coverage for every participant.

The pooled EOG-guided ICA envelope's 5/10 result is unusual under the frozen-score rank model: P(K≤5)=0.00884173, with 100,000 tied-score reassignments giving 0.00857. Four failures are driven by N170 decoding and one by P3 voltage. This ICA is distinct from the per-record ICLabel recipes in the target intervention. Five development-subset refits change mean P3 from 3.02 to 8.09 µV and N170 frozen BA from 53.44% to 60.81%, connecting fit sensitivity to the measured endpoints. This is a practical sensitivity clue, **not a demonstration that exchangeability breaks**: conditional on one frozen operator, instability across other operators does not invalidate the rank guarantee. The refits may change fit/test roles and are not a fresh coverage study. Rare allocation, participant heterogeneity and procedure dependence remain unresolved.

## D3. Stage differences and transfer fractions

For each participant, direct reference shift D=ℓ(T₁−T₀), learned shift L=ℓ(F₁−F₀), and residual E=L−D. The transfer fraction is mean(L)/mean(D), **not** mean(L/D). All paired participant data are resampled together using10000 draws, seed 94810. Intervals are descriptive and conditional on fitted operators and the inspected cohort; there is no multiplicity correction. Near-zero or crossing reference-shift denominators make a ratio unstable; the sign diagnostic and denominator interval are retained in the machine-readable record. Residuals can offset or amplify shifts; the ratio need not lie between0 and1 in general. Parent-supervised attenuation is a separate learner/deployment term and must not be attributed to the changed targets. Main-text total recipe-versus-parent intervals retain the original bootstrap realizations in Parts B and C (seed 94709); added stage and ratio intervals use the separately frozen seed 94810. Recomputed versions of the earlier contrasts are recorded here, rather than relabeled as a new replication.
